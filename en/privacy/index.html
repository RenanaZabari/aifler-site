Privacy policy

Updated: October 12, 2026

This is an English translation of the Hebrew privacy policy. If there is any difference between them, the Hebrew version applies.

Who we are

AiFler is a trade name of Indy Enterprises Ltd, company no. 513540484, 4 Ravid St., Alfei Menashe 4485100, Israel. For privacy questions: infoaiflear@gmail.com.

What we collect on the website

When you leave your email to get an agent, we keep it to send you the download link, and updates only if you ticked that you want them. The email isn't sold, and isn't passed to anyone else for their use.

The form where you leave your email belongs to smoove, our mailing service. The email is stored with them, and the link is sent to you from there. The details are in smoove's privacy policy.

There is no legal obligation to give us your email. Without it, we simply can't send you the download link.

When you contact us by email or phone, we use the details only to answer you. About WhatsApp, see the section "Conversations on WhatsApp".

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use cookies, and has no tracking or advertising code - no Meta pixel and no Google Analytics. If we add code like that, we'll update this page in advance. The Penny and Sammy pages embed the smoove form, which may store cookies of its own so that the form works.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it logs the IP address of visitors, for security and operations. The details are in GitHub's privacy policy.

Conversations on WhatsApp

On WhatsApp you may get replies from the AiFler bot, and it says so in its very first message. Renana sees the conversations and can step into any conversation and answer herself. The bot writes its answers with AI, and they may be inaccurate. For anything important, Renana is available.

To understand a message and reply to it, the text goes to Claude by Anthropic, and according to its terms, Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. The messages themselves pass through WhatsApp by Meta, under its privacy policy, and also through the messaging service we use.

We keep the conversation and what you shared in it: name, business name, phone number and what you're interested in. The details are used only to answer you and get back to you, and aren't sold or passed to anyone else for their use. They're kept for no more than 12 months from the last message. After that, the name, phone number and business name you gave are deleted, and only the content of the conversation remains.

What we don't see

Sammy and Penny run on your side, inside your own Claude or ChatGPT account. No bank statements, recordings, transcripts or summaries reach us. Whatever is processed by the AI provider is subject to your agreement with them and to the privacy settings in your account.

Penny

She has no connection to your bank account. She works only on files you give her, and saves the data only in the folder you chose: on your computer, or in your Google Drive. To analyze, the content of the statement is processed by the AI provider you chose (Claude, ChatGPT or Codex), according to the privacy settings in your account.

Sammy

The transcription happens on your computer, and the recordings don't leave it. Claude writes the summary in your account, so the transcript text is processed by Anthropic, according to the privacy settings in your account. The recording, transcript and summary files are saved only on your computer, and AiFler doesn't receive them. The transcription engine itself connects to the internet only for the installation and for a one-time download of the speech model.

Your rights and requests

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, you may ask to see the information we hold about you, ask to correct it, and in some cases ask to delete it.

Want us to delete your email from the list? Write to infoaiflear@gmail.com, and we'll delete it.

Security

We keep very little information, and even that in secure services with limited access. Some of the services we use, such as Anthropic, Meta and GitHub, may store information outside Israel.

Updates

If the policy changes, the new version will appear here with a date.
