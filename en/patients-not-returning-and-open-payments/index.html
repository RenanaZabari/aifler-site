How do I follow up on patients who didn't come back and on open payments, without chasing?

An AI agent can go through the clinic's data, find patients who didn't come back for a check-up and payments that are still open, and prepare a draft message for each one. The team approves, and only then does the message go out.

That way the follow-up happens without the receptionist having to remember, and without a message going out to a patient that nobody has seen.

Why does follow-up fall through in a busy clinic?

In a clinic there is sometimes one receptionist, and sometimes the dental assistant too, who does everything: answers WhatsApp, chases patients who didn't come back and payments, orders supplies and sorts out forms. When one person does everything, it is hard to also keep a steady follow-up going.

What does Gabi actually do?

Gabi is an agent that works for the clinic team, behind the scenes. On this topic she does two things:

•

Patients who didn't come back for a check-up

She finds who hasn't come back since a date you choose, sorts them by last visit with a phone number next to each one, and words a message for them.

•

Open payments

She prepares a draft reminder for each open payment. The drafts wait for the team's approval before they go out.

Why does the team's approval matter?

Every reminder goes out only after the team has approved it. That way no message reaches a patient without someone on the team having seen it, and the team decides whether and when to reach out.

What else can Gabi do?

Gabi is built around whatever weighs most on your clinic. Beyond follow-up, she can also be built for supplier orders and tracking the stock of materials, for summaries of team meetings and tasks, for reports to the clinic owners (appointments, cancellations and revenue), and for order in documents and forms.

What is needed for this to work?

The agent works from the data you approve for it, and on the intro call we check which data it can work from. It is built to run in the clinic's accounts, and the data stays with you.

No. Every reminder goes out only after the team has approved it.

Where does the agent run, and who sees the data?

No. She backs her up: she takes the office tasks that eat up time every day, and the team is left with what needs a person.
