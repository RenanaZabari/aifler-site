An agent built for your business

A company that sells to dentists has a lot of work that isn't the selling itself: sales reps in the field who need to report, managers chasing updates, and information scattered between the system, WhatsApp and everyone's memory.

And in the clinic: one receptionist, sometimes with the dental assistant, does everything. She answers WhatsApp, chases patients who didn't come back and payments that are still open, orders materials and sorts out forms. And after 8 pm and on weekends, there's nobody to answer.

This behind-the-scenes work, the back office, can be handed to an agent. It works like another member of the office team: it knows the business and the way you work, and you talk to it with the tools you already have.

What an agent like this can do

  • Take a voice message from a sales rep after a visit, and log it in the dentist's file.
  • Prepare a briefing before a visit: what the dentist bought, what dropped, what was agreed last time and what's open in collections.
  • Answer patients on the clinic's WhatsApp, book appointments, and pass anything urgent to the team.
  • Follow up on patients who didn't come back for a check-up and on open payments, and draft a reminder that the team approves before it goes out.
  • Prepare a regular report for the clinic owners: appointments, cancellations and income.
  • Summarize team meetings and client meetings into a Word file: decisions, tasks and open questions.

How it works

  1. An intro call. You tell me what keeps repeating and what takes up the most time.
  2. Scoping. Together we pick one process, and define what the agent does, what data it works from and what it doesn't do.
  3. An agent that works for you. We build it, test it on your data, and hand it over for everyday work.

And every agent follows the same principle: it asks before it guesses, and your data isn't sent anywhere you didn't choose.

Examples of what can be built: Lina, a WhatsApp assistant for field sales reps, Noa, a digital receptionist who answers the clinic's patients, and Gabi, who works for the clinic team behind the scenes.

01 Example agent for a company

🤝Lina - a WhatsApp assistant for your sales team

What she does
Sales reps record a voice message for her after a visit, and she logs it in the dentist's file. Before the next visit she reminds them what the dentist bought, what he stopped buying, and whether there's an open debt.
What you get
  • A post-visit report as a voice message, in Hebrew, with the product names in English. No forms.
  • A briefing before a visit: what was bought, what dropped, what was agreed last time and what's open in collections.
  • A day plan: she helps pick the dentists worth visiting - the ones not seen in a while, the ones whose sales dropped, the ones with a debt - sorts them by distance, and writes next to each one why.
  • A dentist who hasn't bought in a long time: what he bought before he stopped and why he left, so you don't walk in from scratch.
Where she works
WhatsApp, on each sales rep's phone. Behind the scenes she runs on the company's server.
More details: who it's for, privacy and what to know
Who it's for
Companies that sell to dental clinics and have sales reps in the field, and sales managers who want to know what's going on with each dentist without chasing reports.
Privacy
Lina is installed on your server, with your WhatsApp number and your account. The recordings are transcribed on the server, and the sales data stays with you - AiFler doesn't see it. Every number she gives comes from your data, not from the AI model. To understand what was said in a message, the text goes to Claude through the company's account.
Good to know
Lina works from an orders report exported from your system, by clinic, product and quantity. Without an export like that, she can't work.

Price: Depends on the number of sales reps and on your system. We'll talk about it on a call.

02 Example agent for a clinic

🦷Noa - a digital receptionist who answers patients on WhatsApp

What she does
Answers patients in the chat, evenings and weekends too, about hours, prices and preparing for treatment. Books, moves and cancels appointments, and passes to the team anything she doesn't know.
What you get
  • Answers from a single info sheet about the clinic: hours, address, dentists and price ranges.
  • Booking, moving and cancelling appointments in the chat. Patients can write to her or record a voice message.
  • In an emergency, like swelling with fever, trouble breathing or bleeding, she doesn't ask more questions: she passes it straight to the team and refers the patient to the ER or an urgent care center.
  • A daily summary for the team: what was booked, what was passed on and what's urgent.
Where she works
On the clinic's WhatsApp.
More details: who it's for, privacy and what to know
Who it's for
Busy dental clinics where one receptionist, sometimes with the dental assistant, does everything, and Noa backs her up. And clinics whose WhatsApp goes unanswered in the evening and on weekends.
Privacy
To understand a message and reply to it, the text goes to Claude. Noa doesn't ask for detailed medical information, ID numbers or credit card details. To book an appointment she only needs a name, date of birth and phone number, and someone who has already booked with her isn't asked again.
Good to know
Noa doesn't replace a dentist. She doesn't diagnose, doesn't recommend medication, and doesn't promise a final price. Anything she isn't sure about goes to the team, and the appointments she books go to the team for approval. She also doesn't pretend: anyone who asks hears that she's the digital receptionist and that there's a real team behind her.

Price: Depends on the size of the clinic and the setup. We'll talk about it on a call.

04

🎙️Sammy - meeting transcripts and summaries, on your own computer

What he does
Turns a meeting recording, in Hebrew or English, into an accurate transcript and a clear summary in a Word file: decisions, tasks, open questions and quotes.
What you get
  • A clean transcript, and a transcript with timestamps for quotes.
  • A summary that tells someone who wasn't at the meeting exactly what happened: decisions, tasks with an owner and a date, open questions.
  • A Word file, ready to send.
  • A long recording that got interrupted picks up where it stopped.
Where he works
The Claude desktop app (the Code tab), on Windows and Mac. You need about 2GB free, mostly for the speech model that downloads once.
More details: who it's for, privacy and what to know
Who it's for
Managers and sales people who come out of a meeting with a client or the team with a recording and no summary, and anyone who doesn't want to upload work conversations to a cloud service.
Privacy
The transcription happens on your computer, and the recording doesn't leave it. Claude writes the summary in your account, so the transcript text is processed by Anthropic, according to the privacy settings in your account. The files are saved only on your side, and AiFler doesn't see them. No keys and no extra sign-up. Sammy also doesn't make things up: before every summary he asks who was there, and anything unclear goes into "Open questions".
Good to know
Sammy doesn't tell speakers apart, so he asks who was there before every summary. He works only in the Code tab of the Claude desktop app, not in the regular chat and not on claude.ai.

05

💰Penny - a personal companion for your money

What she does
Reads your checking account transactions and your credit card statements, shows where the money goes, and builds a budget with you. In Hebrew, and without judging.
What you get
  • One clear picture of the month from your checking account and all your credit cards, including the subscription you forgot about.
  • A heads-up before you overspend, not after, both on your spending pace and on the lowest point of your checking account.
  • A goal that turns into a savings plan, without touching your safety reserve.
  • Every confusing term explained in two sentences.
Where she works
Claude (the free plan too), ChatGPT on a paid plan (Plus and up), and Codex on a plan that includes Codex.
More details: who it's for, privacy and what to know
Who it's for
Anyone who finally wants to see where the money goes, anyone who finds financial terms confusing, and anyone who wants their financial data to stay with them.
Privacy
There's no connection to your bank account at all. Penny works only on the files you download from the bank's website and give her, and keeps the numbers only in the folder you chose: on your computer, or in your Google Drive. To analyze them, Claude, ChatGPT or Codex process the statement according to the privacy settings in your account. A transaction she doesn't recognize, she doesn't guess - she asks.
Good to know
Penny isn't an investment advisor or an accountant, and doesn't take any action at the bank. She also doesn't run in the background: there are no automatic alerts. She's there when you open her, and only then.

Price: Free gift

Penny herself is free on every platform. ChatGPT and Codex need their own paid plan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an agent for a business cost?

It depends on the scope: how many people work with it, which system it gets data from, and what it needs to do. We'll talk about it on the intro call.

Where does the agent run, and who sees the data?

The agent is built to run in your business's accounts, and the data is stored with you. To understand messages and reply, the text goes to Claude through your account. I wrote the details for each agent on its card.

Do we need to switch systems?

No. The agent works from the data you already have, for example an orders report exported from your system. On the intro call we'll check what can be exported from it.

Do you build agents for businesses outside the dental world too?

Yes. My main focus is dental clinics and dental companies, but an existing agent can be adapted to another business too. For example, Lina can be adapted to any company with sales reps in the field, in any industry. And an agent can also be built for a store or an office. On the intro call we'll check whether it's a fit.

Do the agents work in English?

The agents work in Hebrew. If your business needs another language, tell me on the intro call.

Are Sammy and Penny really free?

Yes. Sammy works in the Claude desktop app. Penny works in Claude, the free plan too, in ChatGPT on a paid plan (Plus and up), and in Codex on a plan that includes Codex.

Do I need technical knowledge to install them?

No. The installation follows steps with screenshots, with no coding. Sammy installs whatever is missing on your computer by himself, after you approve, and Penny explains every term in plain language. Both work on Windows and on Mac.
