AI agent or receptionist?

An AI agent for a clinic is not a new receptionist. It is an extra team member that takes the tasks that repeat and the hours when nobody is there to answer, and the receptionist stays.

The receptionist does what needs presence and judgment. The agent does what repeats every day, and what nobody has time for.

What happens in the clinic today?

In many clinics one receptionist, and sometimes the assistant too, does everything. She answers WhatsApp, chases patients who didn't come back and open payments, orders supplies and sorts out forms. And after 8 PM and on weekends there is nobody to answer.

What is an AI agent for a clinic?

An agent is an assistant built for your business. It knows the clinic and the way you work, and works in the tools you already have, like the clinic's WhatsApp. It works like an extra team member in the office.

I build it around whatever weighs on you most. Here are two examples:

Does an agent replace a receptionist?

No. An agent gives the receptionist backup. It handles the hours when nobody is there and the tasks that repeat, and she stays with what needs a person: a worried patient, a decision in an unusual case, an approval before anything goes out.

Every collections reminder goes out only after the team approved it. Appointments the agent books reach the team for approval. Anything it isn't sure about goes to the team.

What doesn't an agent do?

An agent in a clinic doesn't replace a dentist. It doesn't diagnose, doesn't recommend medication and doesn't promise a final price. And it doesn't pretend: anyone who asks it hears that it is the digital receptionist and that there is a real team behind it.

What happens in an urgent case?

In an urgent situation, like swelling with a fever, difficulty breathing or bleeding, the agent doesn't keep asking questions. It passes the case to the team immediately and refers the patient to the ER or a hotline.

What happens to the clinic's information?

The agent is built to run in your business's own accounts, and the information stays with you. To understand messages and reply, the text goes to Claude through your account.

And every agent follows the same principle: it asks before it guesses, and your information is not sent to places you didn't choose.
