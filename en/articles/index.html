Straight answers to the questions dental clinic owners and dental companies ask about AI agents.

General 🤖

What is an AI agent, and how is it different from a chatbot?

An AI agent is a digital assistant that receives a task and works on the information and tools it is connected to. A chatbot, usually, only answers what it is asked in the conversation.

AI agent or receptionist?

An AI agent for a clinic is not a new receptionist. It is an extra team member that takes the tasks that repeat and the hours when nobody is there to answer, and the receptionist stays.

Patient data and AI: what to ask every vendor before bringing them into your clinic

Before you bring an AI tool into a clinic, it is worth asking every vendor five questions: where the agent runs, who sees the information, where the text goes, what information it asks patients for, and what it does without the team's approval.

How do I follow up on patients who didn't come back and on open payments, without chasing?

An AI agent can go through the clinic's data, find patients who didn't come back for a check-up and payments that are still open, and prepare a draft message for each one. The team approves, and only then does the message go out.

How to start with AI in a clinic: one process first

The simplest way to start with AI in a clinic is to choose one process that keeps repeating, and define what the agent does in it, which data it works from and what it doesn't do.

Read ← General 🔌

Do you need to replace the clinic's software to use AI?

No. An AI agent works with the data you already have and in the tools you already use, and it doesn't replace your system.

Read ← For dental clinics 💬

Do patients know they are talking to AI?

Yes. An AI agent that answers patients doesn't pretend to be a person: if you ask it, it tells you it's the digital receptionist and that a real team stands behind it.

How does an AI agent help field sales reps at a dental company?

An AI agent for a dental company is a WhatsApp assistant where field reps report a visit in a voice message, and it logs it in the dentist's file. Before the next visit it prepares a briefing for them: what the dentist bought, what they stopped buying, and what's open in collections.
