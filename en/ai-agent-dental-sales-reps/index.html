How does an AI agent help field sales reps at a dental company?

An AI agent for a dental company is a WhatsApp assistant where field reps report a visit in a voice message, and it logs it in the dentist's file. Before the next visit it prepares a briefing for them: what the dentist bought, what they stopped buying, and what's open in collections.

The result: the manager knows what's happening with each dentist without chasing reports, and the rep arrives at the visit prepared.

Where does the time go in a company that sells to dentists?

A company that sells to dentists has a lot of work that isn't the selling itself: field reps who need to report, managers who chase updates, and information scattered between the system, WhatsApp and everyone's memory.

This work, which happens behind the scenes, can be handed to an agent. It works like another member of the office team: it knows the business and the way you work, and you talk to it with the tools you already have.

What does an agent like this do for field reps?

I call this agent Lina. She helps on WhatsApp, on the phone of each field rep:

🎙️

Reporting after a visit

You record a voice message, in Hebrew with the product names in English, and she logs it in the dentist's file. No forms.

📋

A briefing before a visit

What the dentist bought, what dropped, what was agreed last time and what's open in collections.

🗺️

A day plan

She helps choose which dentists are worth visiting: those who haven't been seen in a while, those whose sales dropped, those with a debt. She arranges them by distance, and writes next to each one why.

•

A dentist who hasn't bought in a long time

What they bought before they stopped and why they left, so you don't walk in from scratch.

Lina is an example of an agent built for a company. Each agent is built to fit the company in front of me.

What does the manager get?

Sales managers want to know what's happening with each dentist without chasing reports. When the report is logged in the dentist's file right after the visit, the information is in one place and not in everyone's memory.

Where does the agent get its numbers?

Lina connects to your system according to the permissions you approve. Every number she gives comes from your data and not from the AI model.

It's up to you: she works only with the information you approved for her. On the intro call we decide together which information she connects to.

What happens to the company's data?

All the data stays with you, in private. The agent is installed on the company's server, and stays private and with you the whole time. The sales data stays with you, and AiFler doesn't see it. To understand a message, the text goes to Claude through the company's account.

Who is this for?

Companies that sell through field sales reps, and sales managers who want to know what's happening with each dentist without chasing reports.
