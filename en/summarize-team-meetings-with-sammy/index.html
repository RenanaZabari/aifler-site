How to summarize a team meeting without taking notes: Sammy

Sammy turns a recording of a meeting, in Hebrew or English, into a transcript and a tidy summary in a Word file: decisions, tasks, open points and quotes.

Sammy is a gift: you can download him and try him yourself, and the transcription runs on your computer.

What's in the summary?

•

Decisions

What was decided in the meeting.

•

Tasks

With an owner and a date.

•

Open points

What wasn't settled.

•

Quotes

Also with timestamps.

What happens to the recording?

The transcription runs on your computer, and the recording doesn't leave it. The summary is written by Claude in your account, so the transcript text is processed at Anthropic, according to the privacy settings in the account. The files are saved only with you, and AiFler doesn't see them.

What do you need to run Sammy?

Sammy works in the Code tab of the Claude desktop app, on Windows or Mac. Sammy doesn't work in the regular chat or on the claude.ai website. You need about 2GB free, mostly for the speech model, which is downloaded once. Whatever is missing on your computer, Sammy installs after you approve. The installation follows steps with screenshots, with no coding.

What doesn't Sammy do?

  • He doesn't tell speakers apart, so he asks who was at the meeting before every summary.
  • He doesn't invent decisions: whatever isn't clear to him goes into "open points".

How do you get Sammy?

  1. 1
    Choose an agent. Go to Sammy's page and click "Get Sammy".
  2. 2
    Leave an email. You get a download link and an installation guide with screenshots.
  3. 3
    Give him a first task. Write to Sammy "I have a recording". He introduces himself and asks what he needs to know.
