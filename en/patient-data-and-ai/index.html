Patient data and AI: what to ask every vendor before bringing them into your clinic

Before you bring an AI tool into a clinic, it is worth asking every vendor five questions: where the agent runs, who sees the information, where the text goes, what information it asks patients for, and what it does without the team's approval.

This is how my agents are built, and it is also how you can check any other vendor.

Where does the agent run, and who sees the information?

The agent is built to run in your business's own accounts, and the information stays with you. This is the first question, because it decides who actually holds your patients' information.

Where does the text of the messages go?

To understand a message and reply to it, the text goes to Claude. With me this happens through the clinic's own account. It is worth asking every vendor: which AI service does the text go to, and through which account?

What information does the agent ask patients for?

Noa, for example, doesn't ask for detailed medical information, an ID number or credit card details. To book an appointment she only needs a name, a date of birth and a phone number, and someone who has already booked with her isn't asked again.

What does the agent do without the team's approval?

  • A payment reminder goes out only after the team approved it.
  • An appointment the agent books reaches the team for approval.
  • Anything the agent isn't sure about goes to the team.
  • In an urgent case it passes the case to the team immediately and refers the patient to the ER or a hotline.

A list of questions for every vendor

  • Where does the agent run, and in which account?
  • Who sees the information, and does the vendor itself see it?
  • Where does the text go so the agent can understand and reply?
  • What information does the agent ask patients for, and what doesn't it ask for?
  • Which actions need the team's approval?
  • What doesn't the agent do? (diagnosis, medication, a final price)

This is a list of questions, not legal advice. For what the law requires regarding medical information, it is worth checking with the clinic's legal advisor as well.

How do you start?

On the intro call we define together what the agent does, what information it works from and what it doesn't do. And every agent follows the same principle: it asks before it guesses, and your information is not sent to places you didn't choose.
