What Sammy does

From a raw recording to a document you can send

Drag a recording into the folder and tell Sammy you have a recording. When the transcript is ready, ask him to summarize the meeting.

  1. 01

    Accurate transcription

    Clean text, text with timestamps for quotes, and a properly aligned Word file. A long recording that got interrupted picks up where it stopped.

  2. 02

    An in-depth summary

    Decisions, tasks with an owner and a date, open questions and quotes. Someone who wasn't at the meeting will understand exactly what happened in it.

  3. 03

    Asks before he guesses

    Before every summary Sammy asks who was at the meeting. He doesn't make up decisions, and anything unclear goes into "Open questions".

Privacy

Your meetings stay with you

Cloud transcription services upload your recording to someone else's server. Sammy doesn't. The transcription engine (Whisper) runs on your computer, and the recording doesn't leave it. Claude writes the summary in your account, so the transcript text is processed by Anthropic, according to the privacy settings in your account. The files are saved only on your side, and AiFler doesn't see them.

The recording doesn't go to the cloud

The transcription happens on your computer, and the recording, transcript and summary files are saved only there.

No keys and no accounts

Nothing needs a sign-up except Claude itself.

Internet only twice

For the installation, and for a one-time download of the speech model. After that, transcription works offline too.

Doesn't delete old transcripts

They move to an archive, and transcribing again keeps the previous version aside.

Questions before you download

Where does Sammy work?

Only in Claude Code: the Code tab in the Claude desktop app. Not in the regular chat, and not on claude.ai in the browser. There he runs in the cloud and not on your computer, so he can't transcribe your recordings.

Does Sammy know who said what?

No. The transcription engine doesn't tell speakers apart. That's why Sammy asks who was at the meeting before every summary, and doesn't attribute sentences to people based on a guess.

How long does a transcription take?

It depends on the computer. The first transcription starts with downloading the speech model, which takes a few minutes. A long recording can be transcribed overnight: Sammy can set that up, but only if you ask.

What if something doesn't work?

Ask Sammy to check that everything is OK. He runs a check that tells you at which stage something got stuck, without touching any of your recordings. Stuck? Write to infoaiflear@gmail.com with the output of the check.
