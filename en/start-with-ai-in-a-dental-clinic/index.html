How to start with AI in a clinic: one process first

The simplest way to start with AI in a clinic is to choose one process that keeps repeating, and define what the agent does in it, which data it works from and what it doesn't do.

You don't start with the whole clinic at once. You start with the one thing that takes the most time, and build from there.

How do you choose the first process?

A good process to start with meets several of these:

  • It repeats every day or every week.
  • It takes up the team's time.
  • There is data the agent can work from.
  • There is a point where the team approves before anything goes out.

Which processes suit an agent?

These are examples of processes an agent can take on in a clinic:

  • Answering patients on WhatsApp, in the evening and on weekends too, and booking appointments.
  • Following up on patients who didn't come back for a check-up.
  • Collections follow-up, with a draft reminder that the team approves.
  • Supplier orders and tracking the stock of materials.
  • Summaries of team meetings and tasks.
  • A regular report for the clinic owners: appointments, cancellations and revenue.
  • Order in documents and forms, for example consent to treatment.

What does it look like, step by step?

  1. 1
    An intro call. You tell me what keeps repeating and what takes up the most time.
  2. 2
    Scoping. Together we choose one process, and define what the agent does, which data it works from and what it doesn't do.
  3. 3
    An agent that works for you. I build it, test it on your data, and hand it over for day-to-day work.

What does the agent need from you?

It depends on the process. Noa, for example, answers from a single information sheet about the clinic: hours, address, doctors and price ranges. An agent that follows up on patients who didn't come back needs access to the visit data, according to what you approve.

What happens after the agent starts working?

The agent works like an extra team member in the office: it knows the business and the way you work, and you talk to it with the tools you already have. Whatever it isn't sure about goes to the team, and whatever goes out, like a payment reminder, goes out only after approval.

Questions and answers

Where do I start with AI in a clinic?

You choose one process that keeps repeating, and define what the agent does in it, which data it works from and what it doesn't do.

Do I need to replace my system?

No. The agent works from the data you already have. On the intro call we'll check what can be exported from it.

How much does an agent for a clinic cost?

It depends on the scope: how many people work with it, which system it gets data from, and what it needs to do. We'll talk about it on the intro call.

What happens on the intro call?

You tell me what keeps repeating and what takes up the most time, and I check with you what can be built.

Does the agent replace the team?

No. It takes the tasks that keep repeating, and the team is left with what needs judgment.
