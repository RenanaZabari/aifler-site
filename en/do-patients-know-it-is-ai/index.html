Do patients know they are talking to AI?

Yes. An AI agent that answers patients doesn't pretend to be a person: if you ask it, it tells you it's the digital receptionist and that a real team stands behind it.

Here is what the agent does, and what it doesn't do, when a patient writes to the clinic on WhatsApp.

What does Noa say when a patient asks who she is?

Noa is a digital receptionist who answers patients on the clinic's WhatsApp. She doesn't pretend to be someone else: if you ask, she says she's the digital receptionist and that a real team stands behind her.

What does she answer?

  • Hours, address, doctors and price ranges, from a single information page about the clinic.
  • Preparation for treatment.
  • Booking, moving and canceling appointments in the chat. You can write to her or record a voice message.

She also answers in the evening and on weekends, when there's usually nobody to answer.

What happens in an urgent situation?

In an urgent situation, such as swelling with a fever, difficulty breathing or bleeding, Noa doesn't ask more questions. She passes it to the team right away and directs the patient to the ER or an emergency line.

What doesn't Noa do?

Noa doesn't replace a doctor. She doesn't diagnose, doesn't recommend medication and doesn't promise a final price. Anything she isn't sure about goes to the team, and the appointments she books reach the team for approval.

What does the team see at the end of the day?

Noa prepares a daily summary for the team: what was booked, what was passed on and what's urgent. That way the team knows what happened during the hours it wasn't available, and which conversation is waiting for it.

How do we start?

On an intro call you tell me what takes the most time at the clinic. We build an agent together, test it on your information, and only then hand it over for everyday work.

What information does Noa ask a patient for?

Not detailed medical information, an ID number or credit card details. To book an appointment she only needs a name, a date of birth and a phone number.
