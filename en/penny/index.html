Questions before you download

Does Penny have access to my bank account?

No. There's no connection to the account at all. You download the statements from the bank's or credit card company's website (CSV, Excel or PDF), and give them to Penny. She doesn't access anything by herself.

Is it only for the bank account, or for credit cards too?

For cards too, and mainly. In the checking account you see only one line of "card charge". In each card's statement you see what the money actually went on. Penny reads both together, without counting the same expense twice. Have several cards? Bring them all, otherwise the picture is incomplete.

I have no technical or financial knowledge. Is this for me?

Yes, that's exactly who it's for. Penny explains every term in plain language, doesn't assume prior knowledge, and asks one question at a time.

Where exactly is the data saved?

It depends on where Penny runs. In an app with a folder on your computer (Claude desktop, Codex), she saves to the folder you chose there. In the browser, on claude.ai or in ChatGPT, she has no access to your computer. So she says so up front and lets you choose: a folder in your Google Drive, or working without a fixed folder. She doesn't save to Drive unless you asked.

What if she doesn't recognize a transaction in the statement?

She doesn't guess. A transaction she isn't sure about is marked "uncategorized", and Penny asks you.

Do I need a paid plan?

It depends where. In Claude, Penny works on the free plan too. In ChatGPT you need a paid plan (Plus and up), and in Codex a plan that includes Codex. Penny herself is a free gift, on each of them.

Does it work on a Mac too?

Yes. The installation is done inside the app, so it's almost the same on Windows and on Mac. The guide inside the file covers the small differences.

Will she tell me where to invest?

No, and that's on purpose. Penny isn't an investment advisor or an accountant. She organizes, analyzes and points things out, and the decision stays with you or with a licensed advisor. On taxes she gives an estimate only, and recommends checking with an accountant.

What if something doesn't work?

Open a new chat and say hi to Penny again. A chat that was open before the installation doesn't know her. Stuck? You can reply to the email with the link, or write to infoaiflear@gmail.com, with a screenshot and a short explanation of what isn't working. Just make sure no numbers from your statements show in the screenshot.
